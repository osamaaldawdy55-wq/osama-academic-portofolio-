Summary of an academic article

150–200 words

A short summary of the article I read, in my own words.

In a journal article, Osatuyi and Passerini (2016), information systems researchers, examine how Twitter affects student engagement and academic performance. They are mostly concerned with whether social media, often seen as a classroom distraction, can actually improve learning compared with traditional discussion boards. The authors begin by running a 14-week experiment with 164 university students, who discussed weekly course topics either on Twitter or on a Moodle discussion board. Firstly, they report that the Twitter group achieved a much higher average final grade (94.23) than the Moodle group (78.95). Secondly, they explain that students who participated more actively performed better, and that Twitter users enjoyed its flexibility, whereas many Moodle users found it too structured and dropped out. Thirdly, they note that network analysis showed that students who were well positioned in the conversation network, and who connected with people outside their class, performed better. Osatuyi and Passerini conclude that educators should encourage rather than discourage Twitter in classrooms, because it is a valuable tool for engaging a new generation of learners.

Reference (APA 7)
Osatuyi, B., & Passerini, K. (2016). Twittermania: Understanding how social media technologies impact engagement and academic performance of a new generation of learners. Communications of the Association for Information Systems, 39, 509–528. https://doi.org/10.17705/1CAIS.03923

Self-reflection essay

Final submission

Thirty minutes of solid writing about what I have learned and how I learn best.

During my previous study period, I was studying at Swinburne University in Melbourne, Australia. I was learning different subjects, including mathematics, marketing, communication, and technology. This experience helped me understand university life and improve my knowledge and skills. It also helped me think more about my future career and what I want to achieve.

I chose to study because I want to develop my skills and build a successful future. I am interested in technology, especially the Internet of Things (IoT), because it can make people's lives easier. For example, technology can help people with disabilities communicate and become more independent. I believe this is an important field because technology is becoming a big part of our daily lives. I feel that I am studying something useful for my future.

I was happy with some of my results, but I also faced some challenges. Mathematics and academic writing were sometimes difficult for me because they required more practice and concentration. English was another challenge because I sometimes found it difficult to express my ideas clearly. However, I tried my best, completed my tasks, and listened to my teachers. I learned that making mistakes is part of learning and that I should not give up when something is difficult.

Based on my previous study period, I want to improve the way I organise my time. Sometimes, I did not manage my study time well, especially when I had several assignments. This time, I plan to follow a weekly study schedule, start assignments earlier, and review my notes regularly. I also want to participate more in classroom discussions and become more confident when speaking in front of other students. I believe these changes will help me achieve better results.

In conclusion, my previous study period was a valuable learning experience. I learned new information, developed important skills, and understood my strengths and weaknesses. Although I faced some difficulties, they helped me become more responsible and motivated. This time, I want to work harder, manage my time better, and improve my communication skills to achieve my academic and future career goals.
